Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2024

Barcode Generator Pro is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles information.

1. Information Collection

Offline Functionality: Barcode Generator Pro is designed to work primarily offline. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data (such as your name, email address, or contact details) to external servers.

Barcode Data: The text or numbers you enter to generate barcodes are processed locally on your device. This data is stored in a local database on your phone only if you choose to "Save to Gallery." We do not have access to this information.

2. Permissions

To provide its core features, the app requires the following permissions:

  • Storage/Photos: Required to save the generated barcode images to your device's gallery.
  • Internet: Used only for basic functions like opening this privacy policy or redirecting you to the Play Store for ratings.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app may contain links to external sites (like the Google Play Store). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to read their privacy statements.

4. Data Security

Since all data remains on your device, the security of your information depends on the security of your mobile device. We recommend using device-level security features (like PIN or biometrics) to protect your data.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
